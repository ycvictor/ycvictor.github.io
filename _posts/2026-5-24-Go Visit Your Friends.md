# Go Visit Your Friends

One of my favorite parts of traveling through Europe was spending a few days in Paris with a friend I've known for years. Besides saving quite a bit on accommodation, staying at a friend's place gave me something that no hotel ever could.

Every city feels different when someone who actually lives there shows you around. Instead of only visiting the famous landmarks, I got to experience the rhythm of everyday life. We caught up over meals and walked through neighborhoods that probably wouldn't have made it onto my itinerary. It felt less like being a tourist and more like briefly becoming part of the city.

What I appreciated even more was the chance to reconnect.

It's surprisingly easy for close friends to drift apart as life takes everyone in different directions. Careers, school, moving to different countries, and everyday responsibilities slowly replace the conversations that used to happen so naturally.

Spending time together in Paris gave us the opportunity to catch up on everything that had happened over the years. We reflected on old memories with a more mature perspective, laughed about things that once felt so important, and talked about how much we've both changed.

Somewhere in those conversations, I was reminded of just how quickly time passes. The years between seeing each other in person didn't feel that long until we started piecing together everything that had happened since.

If you happen to come across this post and we're friends, feel free to ask me where I am:)
